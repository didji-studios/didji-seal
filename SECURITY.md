# Reporting a security problem

If you think you have found a security problem in DidjiSeal, `didji-open`, the formats in SPEC.md, or
the Didji app, please tell us privately first.

- **How**: GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" on this repository (Security, Advisories), or email
  hello@didji.app.
- **Scope**: this package, `didji-open`, the formats, and the Didji app's handling of keys and
  backups.
- **We will reply within five working days**, keep you told while we fix it, and agree with you when
  it is made public.
- **We will not take legal action** against anyone who reports in good faith, does not access other
  people's data, and gives us reasonable time to fix the problem before telling others.
- **With your permission we will thank you by name**, here and in the release notes. We cannot offer
  payment for reports at present.

## Supported versions

The latest version of DidjiSeal (CHANGELOG.md) and the Didji app's current release on the App Store.
A fix goes into a new version of both; older versions are not patched separately, and every version
still opens what earlier ones wrote.

Didji is made by Didji Studios Ltd.
